Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

Summary: EventDesk is designed to keep event information on the device where the app is used. FlutterBee Labs does not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

EventDesk is developed by FlutterBee Labs in Ontario, Canada. Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@flutterbeelabs.ca.

2. Information EventDesk may store

Depending on how an organizer uses EventDesk, the app may store event names, dates, locations, participant names and contact details entered by the organizer, competition settings, scores, standings, prize or settlement information, credit-ledger records and generated event/report information.

3. Where event data is stored

The current EventDesk application is designed around a local application database on the user’s device. FlutterBee Labs does not currently operate a cloud account service that automatically uploads an organizer’s EventDesk event database to FlutterBee Labs servers.

4. Purchases and Event Credits

When Event Credits are purchased through a supported app store, the store provider processes the payment and provides EventDesk with purchase information needed to fulfil the credit purchase. FlutterBee Labs does not receive full payment-card details from the app store.

5. Support communications

If you contact FlutterBee Labs for support, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device/platform information, screenshots, error details and a description of the issue. We use that information to respond to your request and improve support.

6. Advertising and sale of data

FlutterBee Labs does not sell personal information. EventDesk is not designed around third-party advertising.

7. Reports and sharing

EventDesk may allow organizers to generate or share reports containing event and participant information. The organizer controls when and with whom those reports are shared and should only share information they are authorized to disclose.

8. Retention and deletion

Local event information remains on the device until it is removed through EventDesk features, the app’s data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to platform behaviour and backups controlled by the device owner. Support emails and related correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain business records and resolve issues.

9. Children

EventDesk is an organizer tool and is not intended to be directed to young children. Organizers are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to enter participant information.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to design EventDesk and our business systems with security in mind. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as EventDesk changes, including if cloud services, analytics or additional platform services are introduced. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
